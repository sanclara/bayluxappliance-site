Editorial Policy

BayLuxAppliance maintains the following standards for every page published on this site.

  • Pricing ranges are sourced from published consumer research (for example ConsumerAffairs, Airtasker, and Sears Home Services) and are updated when source data changes.
  • Licensing and regulatory statements reference California's Bureau of Household Goods and Services and the federal EPA Section 608 program, current as of the publish date shown on each page.
  • General industry figures (appliance lifespans, typical repair cost ranges, California licensing requirements) are sourced from the public references above and updated as source data changes.
  • No affiliate links are present on this site.
