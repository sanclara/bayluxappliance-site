FAQs

Is DIY appliance repair a good idea in Santa Clara?

DIY repair can void a manufacturer warranty and is not always covered by homeowner insurance. It works for minor fixes only, such as replacing a washing machine lid switch, but gas and electrical appliance faults carry safety risk best left to a licensed technician.

Should I choose OEM or generic parts for my appliance repair?

OEM parts cost more but preserve the manufacturer warranty on brands like GE, Samsung, and Sub-Zero. Generic third-party parts cost less upfront but carry inconsistent warranty coverage and can void the original manufacturer warranty.

Should I repair or replace my appliance?
